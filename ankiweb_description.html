Background Sync keeps Anki in sync with AnkiWeb automatically while it is open, with no progress window and no interruptions. Features
  • Syncs every 5 minutes (adjustable from 2 to 240).
  • Never interrupts you: if you are studying, it waits until Anki has been idle for 60 seconds (adjustable) and until the question side is showing.
  • Only does a real sync when something changed, here or on AnkiWeb (it first does the same quick check Anki uses to colour the Sync button).
  • Uses Anki's own sync, so media follow your "Sync audio and images too" preference.
  • If AnkiWeb requires a full sync, Anki's usual dialog asks you whether to upload or download. If you cancel, it stops asking until you sync manually.
  • Offline? It shows a small warning and retries by itself, without popups.
  • A small ☁ item in the top toolbar shows the time of the last sync. Click it to sync now, pause for 1 hour, pause until restart, or open the settings.
  • English, Spanish and French.
Settings: Tools → Background Sync… (or the Config button). Requires Anki 23.10 or newer, and being logged in to AnkiWeb. Source code, issues and changelog: github.com/jorgechachaima-collab/anki-background-sync License: AGPL-3.0-or-later.
